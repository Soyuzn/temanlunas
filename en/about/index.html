Three words, one promise.

Each word in our name is how we work with you.

  • Solusi

    Solution: we look for a realistic way forward that fits your situation.

  • Teman

    Companion: we stay with you patiently, without pressure or judgement.

  • Lunas

How we work.

Once an account is transferred to us, STL manages communication with the client, directly or through professional collection partners we supervise.

  1. Taking over portfolios

    We buy receivables from banks, fintechs, and financial institutions, officially and on record.

  2. Reaching out politely

    Our team or professional partners contact clients only through official channels.

  3. Finding a way to settle

    We explain the available options and record every agreement.

Principles in every conversation.

  • Respectful

    No threats, no shame.

  • Clear

    Plain language, never misleading.

  • Safe

    Data protected and used only as needed.

  • Fair

    Solutions found together, based on real circumstances.
